13. FAQ Section

FAQ is important because renewable energy has many questions. Use accordion style.

Questions About Renewable Energy?


FAQ Items
What does Stellora offer?


Stellora offers renewable energy solutions including solar power, wind energy, hybrid systems, battery storage, consultation, monitoring, and maintenance.


Is solar or wind energy better for my property?


It depends on your location, available space, sunlight, wind flow, energy usage, and budget. A consultation helps identify the best solution.


Can solar and wind work together?


Yes. A hybrid system can combine solar and wind energy to create a more flexible renewable power setup.


Do I need battery storage?


Battery storage is useful if you want backup power, night time energy use, or better control over stored electricity.


How do I get started?


Start with a consultation. Stellora can review your energy needs and recommend the right clean energy solution.

Recommended Homepage Visual Style
Layout
Use a modern editorial layout with large typography, clean cards, and floating data blocks. Your references show strong hero areas with big text, image depth, glass cards, and strong CTA buttons. That style fits Stellora well.

Colors
Use your brand colors like this:

Dark Green: Hero text, footer, dark sections, primary buttons
Lime Green: CTA highlights, icons, badges, hover states
Soft Cream: Main page background
White: Cards and text contrast
Light Green: Section backgrounds and decorative shapes

Typography
Use your brand font strongly in headings. Make the hero headline large and confident. You can use a clean sans serif as the main font and an italic serif accent only for selected words if you want a premium editorial touch.
Example:

Powering Tomorrow with Solar and Wind Energy


You could make “Tomorrow” or “Energy” in italic serif style if it matches your guideline.

Image Direction
Use:

Solar panels close up
Wind turbines in open fields
Engineers installing panels
Green hills and sky
Battery storage systems
Clean energy infrastructure

Avoid using too many random nature photos without energy context. Every image should connect to solar, wind, power, installation, or sustainability.
